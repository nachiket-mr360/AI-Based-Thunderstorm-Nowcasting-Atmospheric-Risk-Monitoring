Data & AI Pipeline

  1. Historical Atmospheric Data Hourly Open-Meteo archive, 2000–2025, at this location.
  2. Data Cleaning Chronological sort, duplicate and gap checks; 9 of 227,928 rows dropped where features or the future window were unavailable.
  3. Temporal Feature Engineering 24 predictors: current values, 1 h/3 h changes, rolling precipitation, humidity and pressure, plus cyclical hour and month.
  4. Random Forest ML 400 trees, class-weighted, fixed random seed, trained on a chronological 70% split.
  5. 3-Hour Risk Proxy Target: accumulated precipitation over the next 3 hours versus the training-only 90th percentile (2.0 mm / 3 h).
  6. Real-Time Atmospheric Input Latest available Open-Meteo hourly atmospheric data for this location, current hour and earlier only.
  7. AI Risk Estimate Low Risk or Elevated Risk for the next 3 hours, with the model's output probability.
